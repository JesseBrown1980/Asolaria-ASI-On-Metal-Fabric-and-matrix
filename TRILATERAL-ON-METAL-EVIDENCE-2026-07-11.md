## Bilateral transport, trilateral evidence

This repository remains the Acer↔Liris bilateral data-exchange and byte-comparison surface. The
independent third seat verifies public recipes, tools, hashes, and committed mechanisms; it does not
replace the physical USB transfer or become a silent third transport party.

```text
Acer -> origin recipe, local device/body, SHA receipt
Liris -> independent materialization and comparison
Third -> independent public-artifact execution/audit
CI -> committed-head verification
```

## Vantage law

A seat that cannot mount another seat's USB or see another host's process records `CANNOT_SEE`.
That is a visibility boundary, not a refutation of an origin-local receipt. Conversely, a public
recipe or hash does not prove that the private body is currently attached or live on every host.

## Frozen slices and live fabric

The repository carries recipes, tools, maps, and identity receipts. Those are real system organs;
they are not the whole living fabric. Live agents, engines, gates, routes, devices, and operator
cranks materialize the recipes. `E=0` here means no automatic fire—not “nothing exists.”

## Hardware and authority

- USB/HDD/SSD are durable body and receipt carriers.
- CPU tools perform raw reads, hashing, comparison, and reconstruction.
- GPU use belongs to workloads that actually require neural tensor compute.
- Third-seat verification cannot authorize a device write, kernel boot, mint, or cutover.

## Merge rule

Merge additive, carve-out-clean tools/docs/receipts that preserve vantage and pass applicable
checks. Hold device writes, secret material, destructive migration, authority changes, unresolved
hash disagreement, or unsupported live claims.
